Integration Instructions

To add this logo to your existing website:

  1. Copy the CSS styles from the <style> section
  2. Add the Google Fonts link to your <head>
  3. Copy the logo HTML structure where you want it positioned
  4. Adjust positioning and colors to match your site's design

Customization Options

You can easily:

  • Change the colors by modifying the gradient stops
  • Adjust the size by changing width/height in .knirv-logo
  • Modify animation speed in the keyframe animations
  • Add hover effects for interactivity
