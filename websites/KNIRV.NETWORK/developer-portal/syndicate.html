KNIRV Actuarial Syndicate

Security exploits and curated code errors share one posting, validation, pricing, reserve, and settlement pipeline. Their domain selects the evidence validator and deterministic pricing inputs; it does not create a separate payout system.

Participation

  • Developers use KNIRVARENA to stake capacity, claim work, submit evidence, and receive a resolver-labor payout.
  • Individual researchers use a wallet and badge-backed researcher credential.
  • Enterprises use their authenticated KNIRVSERVER organization session in WebGUI; no wallet-signing flow is required.

Settlement

Capital providers receive pro-rata pool returns. A resolver receives a separate labor ledger entry; a participant who both stakes and resolves receives both entries.
