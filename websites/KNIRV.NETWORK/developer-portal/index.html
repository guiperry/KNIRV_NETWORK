What is a DVE?

A Distributed Validation Environment is a sovereign compute container on the KNIRV network. Every DVE is cryptographically attested, governed by user-defined policies, and anchored to KNIRVCHAIN for an immutable audit trail.

Policy Editor

Define governance rules that control how your DVE accepts, executes, and reports on workloads.

Blockchain Commit

Every policy decision and execution result is committed to KNIRVCHAIN, creating a tamper-proof audit trail.

Cryptographic Attestation

Each DVE produces cryptographic proofs of execution — verifiable by any party without trusting the operator.

Cognitive Engine (Pro)

Upgrade to adaptive AI governance — automated policy tuning and anomaly detection without manual intervention.
