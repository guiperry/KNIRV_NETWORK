Core Concepts

KNIRV DVE Architecture

The DVE (Distributed Validation Environment) is the primary service layer of the KNIRV network — a sovereign, policy-governed compute container backed by cryptographic attestation and an immutable KNIRVCHAIN audit trail. The full D-TEN architecture beneath it provides the decentralized infrastructure that makes DVEs verifiable.

The Twelve Layers of D-TEN

The KNIRV D-TEN (Decentralized Trusted Execution Network) provides the infrastructure beneath every DVE. Layer 5 — KNIRV-SERVER DVE — is where developers interact directly: it hosts the Policy Editor, issues validation proofs, and commits results to KNIRVCHAIN.

Key Concepts

DVE

A Distributed Validation Environment is a sovereign compute container with cryptographic attestation, policy governance, and a blockchain-committed audit trail.

Policy Editor

Define governance rules that control how your DVE accepts, executes, and reports on workloads. Policies are versioned and committed on-chain.

Blockchain Commit

Every policy change and execution result is committed to KNIRVCHAIN, producing a tamper-proof audit trail verifiable by any third party.

ErrorNodes

Failed AI attempts that become learning opportunities. KNIRV transforms validation failures into structured training data for the network's collective intelligence.

Cognitive Engine (Pro)

Adaptive AI governance layer that automatically tunes policies and detects anomalies — available on the Professional tier and above.

NRN Tokens

Network Resolution Notices — the native token used to fund DVE compute, reward error resolution, and enable autonomous economic activity.
