The DVE "Solver" Interface

Your CDE is just an editor. Our DVE Interface is a complete problem-solving environment. Load a problem, test your solution, and analyze the validation report—all in one place.

1. The "Problem" Panel: NRV Context Loader

A CDE is a blank slate. A DVE is problem-oriented. Load problems from the network to get started.

  • NRV Browser: Search & filter all open AI failures (NRVs).
  • Context Importer: "Load Problem" button populates failure logs & test harnesses.
  • Skill Manifest: Auto-fills metadata templates for your new solution.

2. The "Validation" Panel: Local Forensics

The "Kali Linux-in-a-box" control panel. Test your Skill in a secure sandbox *before* submission.

  • Run Validation: Executes your Skill against the failure context.
  • Real-Time Log Streams: Live `strace`, `tshark`, and console outputs.
  • Validation Report Card: Get a Pass/Fail summary of static and dynamic analysis.
  • Forensic Analysis: Memory snapshots and filesystem integrity checks.

3. The "Submission" Panel: On-Chain Deployment

Once your Skill passes local validation, this panel handles the final on-chain submission.

  • Skill Signer: Sign the Skill package with your KNIRV-WALLET.
  • Bond Manager: View your NRN balance and the required bond for submission.
  • Submit to Consensus: Submit your code, report, and bond as a transaction.
