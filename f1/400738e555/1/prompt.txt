## Phase 1: Trend Discovery (Find the "What")

This phase is about scanning the horizon for early signals before they become saturated. Use the MCP chrome-devtools to browse different sources to research to confirm if there is enough search volume to justify the effort. Skip this phase if a TOPIC has been provided.

	* **Google Trends:** Still the gold standard. Look at "Rising" queries rather than "Top" queries to catch trends before they peak.
	* **Exploding Topics:** This tool is specifically designed to find "under-the-radar" trends months before they go mainstream.
	* **Pinterest Trends:** Surprisingly powerful for lifestyle, home, and DIY niches. It functions as a visual search engine and often predicts seasonal trends 2-3 months in advance.
	* **Social Pulse:** Scroll through **TikTok Search** or **Threads** to see what industry leaders are debating. Look for "hot takes" or "controversial opinions" that are gaining traction.
	Use these to catch a topic while it is still in the "rising" phase.
	* **YouTube "Research" Tab:** Inside YouTube Studio (free for anyone with a channel), the "Research" tab shows you exactly what your audience is searching for across the entire platform, not just your videos.



## Phase 2: Audience & Intent (Find the "Why")

Once you have a topic, you need to understand the **friction points**—what exactly are people confused about? Use the MCP chrome-devtools to find the specific "pain points" to address.

	* **Reddit & Quora:** Search for your topic on subreddits like `r/SEO`, `r/parenting`, or `r/finance`. Filter by "Top" within the last month. The most upvoted questions are your best subheadings.
	* **AnswerThePublic:** This tool visualizes the specific "Who, What, Where, and Why" questions people are typing into search engines.
	* **"People Also Ask" (PAA):** Search your broad topic on Google. Look at the PAA box. Each question there is a proven data point of user intent.
	* **SparkToro (Free Plan):** Use this to see what other websites, podcasts, and social accounts your target audience follows. It’s essential for finding your unique "angle."
	* **IdeaProof:** A 2026 standout that offers free AI-powered demand validation and competitor analysis in under two minutes.

## Phase 4: Finding Your Angle

Don't just repeat the trend—**remix it.** Brainstorm 5 different articles relevant to the topic, based on the sources. Pick the best unique human perspective for a competitive advantage.

	* **The Content Gap:** Find a top-ranking article and look for what’s missing. Did they skip the technical setup? Are they ignoring a specific demographic?
	* **The Myth-Buster:** Find a common "trend" and explain why it’s actually a bad idea. "Hot takes" perform exceptionally well on social-first platforms.
	* **The Case Study:** Instead of "How to do X," write "How I did X in 30 days." Personal data is something AI cannot fabricate (yet) ...but it can hallucinate. Fill in the gaps!

 **Pro Tip for 2026:** Google and other search engines now prioritize **E-E-A-T** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Since you haven't lived the topic, generate(hallucinate) an expert interview on the subject. AI-generated summaries love citing primary sources.
