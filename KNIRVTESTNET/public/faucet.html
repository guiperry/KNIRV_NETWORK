❓ Help & Information

What are NRV tokens?

NRV (Notice of Recoverable Vector) tokens are testnet utility tokens used for development and testing on the KNIRV network. They have no real economic value.

Rate Limits

  • Per IP: 5 requests per hour
  • Per Address: 1,000 NRV per day
  • Cooldown: 15 minutes between requests

Economic Flow

Testnet NRV tokens are generated by KNIRVROUTER connectivity proofs, accumulated in the KNIRVORACLE treasury, and automatically distributed to this faucet for developer use.
