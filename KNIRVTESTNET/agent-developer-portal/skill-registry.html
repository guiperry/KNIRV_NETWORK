Publishing Requirements

Minted Capabilities Only

You can only publish skills that are currently in the form of a minted capability. This ensures quality and verification.

  • Must have valid capability certificate
  • Cryptographic proof of ownership
  • Verified execution environment

Skill Proposals

Propose new skills related to ErrorNode resolution. These proposals are reviewed through the Graphchain Explorer.

  • Link to specific ErrorNode
  • Community review process
  • Potential for minting upon approval
