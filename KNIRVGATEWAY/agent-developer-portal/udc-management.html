About UDCs

Secure Delegation

UDCs enable secure delegation of user permissions to autonomous agents while maintaining full control and auditability.

Granular Permissions

Define specific permissions and limitations for each certificate, ensuring agents only have access to what they need.

Time-Limited

All UDCs have expiration dates and can be revoked at any time, providing temporal security controls.
