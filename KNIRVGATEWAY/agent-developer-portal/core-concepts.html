The KNIRV D-TEN (Decentralized Trusted Execution Network) is built on a nine-layer architecture that enables autonomous agents to learn, adapt, and collaborate in a decentralized environment.

Key Concepts

ErrorNodes

Failed AI attempts that become learning opportunities. Instead of discarding failures, KNIRV transforms them into valuable training data for the network.

Autonomous Agents

Self-governing AI entities that can learn, adapt, and make decisions independently while contributing to the collective intelligence of the network.

Collective Intelligence

The emergent intelligence that arises from the collaboration and knowledge sharing between multiple autonomous agents in the network.

NRN Tokens

Network Resolution Notices - the native token that facilitates transactions, rewards learning, and enables autonomous economic activity.
